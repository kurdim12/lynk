## Overview (English)

The Lynk & Co 01 is a premium compact plug-in hybrid SUV. It blends a connected,
membership-based ownership model with a refined cabin and an efficient hybrid
powertrain. It is positioned as a smart, design-led alternative for urban and
intercity driving.

وتقدّم خياراً ذكياً وعصرياً للقيادة داخل المدينة وبين المدن.

## Powertrain and Electric Range (English)

The 01 plug-in hybrid pairs a 1.5-litre turbocharged petrol engine with an
electric motor for a combined system output of about 261 horsepower. On a full
charge it delivers an electric-only range of approximately 69 kilometres on the
WLTP cycle, which covers most daily city commutes without using fuel.

## Charging (English)

The high-voltage battery can be charged from a home wall box or a public AC
charger. A typical full charge on a home charger takes a few hours overnight,
making it easy to start each day on electric power.

## Dimensions and Capacity (English)

The 01 seats five passengers. It offers generous luggage space that expands when
the rear seats are folded, suiting both family trips and daily errands.

## الأبعاد والسعة (Arabic)

بما يناسب رحلات العائلة والمهام اليومية على حد سواء.

## Interior and Technology (English)

Inside, the 01 features a large central touchscreen, smartphone connectivity, and
over-the-air software updates. The interior uses sustainable materials and a
minimalist Scandinavian design language.

## Safety (English)

The 01 includes a comprehensive suite of driver-assistance systems, such as
automatic emergency braking, lane-keeping assistance, and adaptive cruise
control, supported by multiple airbags and a high-strength body structure.

## Membership and Ownership (English)

Lynk & Co offers a flexible membership model alongside traditional purchase.
Members get access to connected services and a community of other members. You
can also buy the car outright through an official Lynk & Co channel.

## Warranty (English)

The Lynk & Co 01 comes with a manufacturer warranty of 5 years or 100,000
kilometres, whichever comes first. The high-voltage battery carries a separate,
longer warranty as documented in the official ownership terms.
